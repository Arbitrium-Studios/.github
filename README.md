***
## What is the Arbitrium Studios Organization *.github* Repository?

The *.github* Repository is for the "Arbitrium Studios Organization".

***
### What is Arbitrium Studios?

Arbitrium Studios is the name of the organization founded by Gracie Lovelight that is focused on creating media in the Library of The Fates, websites, and more!

***
### What is the Library of The Fates?

The Library of The Fates (also called "Bibliotheca Fusi" in Latin) is one of the names for the wider continuity of media that is interconnected with media created by Arbitrium Studios! This media includes (but is not limited to) video games, videos, livestreams, and stories!

***
### Who are all the helpers who have helped with the development of our projects?

Arbitrium Studios has a long list of current and former helpers (including staff and contributors). You can find a list of these helpers by heading to the [credits][AS_Website_Credits] page on the [Arbitrium Studios website][AS_Website] as well as [Toontown Fantasy][TTFan_Website]'s [Credits][TTFan_Website_Credits] page!
